## Prompt finale

```text
Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: bespoke photographic artwork for the Italian creative agency Memento Production, displayed in its "Estetica e strategia non sono due reparti separati" website section.
Primary request: create one striking, luxurious editorial still-life photograph showing brand aesthetics and strategic structure becoming one continuous physical composition. A purposeful art-directed photograph, tangible and tactile, not a corporate stock desk scene.
Scene: warm ivory matte studio tabletop, seamless cream backdrop. At the left, a small fan of thick uncoated paper colour swatches in muted forest/sage green #486956, warm ochre gold #D1AC67, dark forest and ivory. The green swatch extends into a beautiful broad, sculptural folded paper ribbon that flows through a single clear curved optical glass prism in the middle. A second narrower matte gold ribbon follows the green ribbon, the two interweaving elegantly. At the right, the ribbons resolve into a precisely aligned editorial composition on thick ivory paper: elegant blank green and gold rectangular fields, fine printed grid lines, a restrained connected sequence of three tiny gold points. The right side should read as thoughtful graphic design planning, not a stock-market chart or fake software dashboard. The central glass optically blends the colours and refracts the planning grid, visually linking spontaneous colour and ordered structure.
Style: real luxury design-magazine product photography, sophisticated physical paper sculpture and optical glass. Low three-quarter overhead camera angle, 50mm lens, carefully controlled studio sunlight from upper left, soft long shadows, a restrained bright caustic at the joining point, crisp paper fibres, believable glass edges and rich matte colour. Warm elegant minimalism.
Composition: wide landscape 3:2 image, one unified continuous arrangement moving diagonally left to right, generously framed so all objects remain inside the central 85% of the image, balanced negative space around them. Strong focal point at the central meeting of ribbons and glass. Foreground and tabletop clean. Harmonious cream, green #486956 and gold #D1AC67 only.
Text: none. No lettering, numbers, labels, hex codes, logos, watermark, captions or typography baked into the image. No people, hands, laptops, phones, paint explosions, glowing neon, floating HUD, arrows, chrome blobs, puzzle pieces, or split-screen collage. Must feel like a crafted premium studio photograph rather than a generic AI business illustration.
```
